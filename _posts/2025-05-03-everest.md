Naturally, I've managed to skip out on entire month of writing, which isn't to say that there wasn't anything accomplished or completed for the entire last month. It seems to be the natural course of those who profess to be writers to avoid writing in general, perhaps suggesting that it's more pleasurable to dream about writing than the actual act of doing so. In my own personal history of writing, this generally means that I've neglected to write things that I've perceived as meaningless or less interesting, especially given the more than public nature of the blog as a writing medium. Despite my beliefs that no one actually reads things here, I have nonetheless been proven wrong. This however shouldn't mean that I neglect the therapeutic nature of writing words into the seemingly empty void, imagined or otherwise.

Thus, into the void we go, and this time with a quite a bit more preemptive thinking than is usual for these sorts of things. My perceived negligence to write can largely be attributed to my complete absence in my daily grind. For the first time in nearly 5 years, I've gone on an actual real vacation. It's quite insane to admit that it's taken that long to feel comfortable enough to wonder beyond the general mundane of life and simply forget about everything else. The reflections of how or why this has happened leads to many trains of thought, all of which I do not have the hope or energy to write for all at once. So, perhaps just one for now.

Perhaps not so ironically, the last vacation that I would have considered real was actually to the same place. Both times, it was Japan. I've actually written about the last trip here as well. I suppose the perceived insanity of it was the long 5 years that seemingly disappeared between the first Japan trip and the recently concluded one. It's fun to think about how the two compare, and perhaps how much or how little has changed in those 5 years. That comparison itself also requires some thinking to reflect on, let alone write about, which is more or less what I'm trying to do here.
